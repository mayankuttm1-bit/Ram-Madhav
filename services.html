Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

Is renting better than buying a home?
Renting is a better option for many who cannot afford to buy a house. Monthly rent is less compared to home loan EMIs. However, buying is always a long-term investment that builds equity. Our consultants can help you decide based on your specific financial situation.
Do I need documents before renting a house?
Yes, proper identity proof and address proof are a must before renting a house. A tenant should also ask for a signed and attested copy of the rent agreement. We assist both tenants and owners with all necessary documentation.
How can I find the best house for rent in Indore?
You can contact Ram Madhav Real Estate & Developers Pvt Ltd for better service and assistance. Our estate agents specialize in finding the best rental properties across Indore's prime locations.
Does Ram Madhav help in tenant verification?
Tenant verification is usually done by the police. However, Ram Madhav assists with the entire verification process, making it smooth and hassle-free for both property owners and tenants.
Do you help in finding PGs and hostels?
Yes! You can contact our estate agents to rent a PG or hostel. We have a complete list of verified properties available for bachelors, students, and working professionals across Indore.
Are your plot titles legally verified?
Absolutely. Every plot we sell comes with clear, verified legal titles and all necessary documentation. We ensure 100% transparency and compliance with all regulations to protect your investment.
Can I schedule a free site visit?
Yes! We offer completely free site visits with pickup and drop facilities. A personal agent will be assigned to guide you through the project. Simply call us at 091112 80169 or fill out our contact form to book your visit.
