Who We Are

India's Emerging Real Estate Powerhouse

Ram Madhav Real Estate & Developers Private Limited is a fast-growing, registered private real estate development firm headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We specialize in premium plotted developments, modern residential projects, and comprehensive property consulting services across Indore's highest-growth corridors.

Formally incorporated under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) guidelines on August 29, 2024, our company is legally governed by the Companies Act as a company limited by shares. We are committed to redefining urban living through legally compliant, strategically located, and aesthetically designed real estate developments.

Our Journey

Our Story

From a bold vision to a trusted real estate brand in Indore — here's how it all began.

The Vision Takes Shape

Directors Ramesh Kushwah and Purnima Kushwah identified a gap in Indore's real estate market — the need for trustworthy, transparent developers offering genuine plots and homes at fair prices. With deep roots in the Indore community and a passion for development, they laid the groundwork for what would become Ram Madhav Real Estate.

Official Incorporation — August 2024

The company was formally registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) with CIN U68100MP2024PTC072700, establishing itself as a legally compliant, private limited company under the RoC-Gwalior jurisdiction.

Launching Flagship Projects

Within months of incorporation, Ram Madhav launched two landmark projects — Grand Riyasat (premium plots at Palakhedi) and Nakshatra Homes (affordable duplex row houses at the Super Corridor) — quickly establishing a footprint in Indore's most strategic growth corridors.

Growing Client Trust

Today, we serve a growing base of happy clients, property investors, and rental seekers across Indore. Our commitment to transparency, legal compliance, and client-first service has positioned us as one of the most trusted emerging real estate brands in the region.
